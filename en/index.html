Galera in English

Galera is a free, open set of tools for people who make books in Spanish, plus a guide to studying Publishing at the University of Buenos Aires (UBA). It is made in Buenos Aires, Argentina, by {{marca.firma_en}}. There are no accounts and no ads: what you enter stays in your browser.

For the book trade: calculators for paper, pagination, costs and schedules; a glossary; a directory of Argentine publishers, printers and bookshops; a calendar of fairs and grants; and guides to copyright law and contracts.

For UBA students: a degree planner with grades, exam dates, timetables without clashes, and study summaries with self-tests.

Most content is in Spanish and covers Argentina; it does not claim to cover other countries. Press: prensa@galera.com.ar. Entrar al sitio, en español.
